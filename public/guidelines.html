Community Guidelines

To keep STEP a safe and effective space for all Brighton students, we ask that you follow these simple principles.

The "Do's"

  • Be Respectful: Treat every student as a colleague.
  • Stay on Topic: Keep discussions focused on module learning.
  • Be Punctual: Value your peer's time for sessions.
  • Provide Feedback: Help your 'Experts' grow with kind reviews.

The "Don'ts"

  • No Academic Misconduct: Do not ask/provide answers for live exams or assignments.
  • No Personal Info: Keep communication within the platform or Brighton email.
  • No Harassment: Zero tolerance for bullying or discriminatory language.
  • No Spam: Do not use the platform for commercial advertising.

A Note on Academic Integrity

STEP is a platform for skills-sharing and guidance. It is not a "cheating service." Experts are here to explain concepts, not to write your essays or solve your final projects. Any evidence of academic dishonesty will result in an immediate account ban.
