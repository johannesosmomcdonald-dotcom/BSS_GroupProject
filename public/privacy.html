Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

We only collect data necessary to facilitate peer mentoring. This includes your name, University of Brighton email address, and the modules you wish to share or learn.

2. Authentication & Security

Access is strictly limited to @uni.brighton.ac.uk account holders. Your university credentials are used to verify your student status and ensure a secure, private environment for all users.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To match 'Learners' with appropriate student 'Experts'.
  • To send session reminders and platform updates to your student email.
  • To maintain a safe community by monitoring adherence to our Guidelines.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third-party advertisers. Your profile information is only visible to other verified Brighton students within the STEP network.

5. Your Rights

You can request to view, edit, or delete your data at any time through your profile settings.
