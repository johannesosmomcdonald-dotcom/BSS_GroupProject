FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

Is STEP free to use?

Yes, it is a peer-to-peer community for all Brighton students.

How do I become an Expert?

Any student can apply through the sign up form. You can choose between being an 'Expert' or Learner.

Do I need to pay for a mentor

No, STEP is a talent exchange platform where students support each other for free to build a stronger academic community.

Who can see my profile?

Only verified University of Brighton students can access the platform; it is a closed, secure network.

What if a session doesn't go well?

We encourage professional feedback. You can report any behavior that violates our Community Guidelines through the 'Report' button.

Can an Expert do my assignment for me?

No. STEP is for skills-sharing and guidance only. Doing work for others is a breach of university academic integrity policies.

How do I know the student 'Expert' actually knows the module?

Experts are verified through their sign up information. A learner can also enquire through a direct message to the expert for more information.

Can I sign up with my personal email?

No. To maintain a secure environment for our students, STEP requires a valid @uni.brighton.ac.uk email address to create an account.

I forgot my password, what do I do?

Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page to receive a reset link to your Brighton email address.

How do I delete my account?

You can deactivate your account at any time through the 'Privacy' section in your profile settings.
